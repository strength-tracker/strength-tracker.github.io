progression rules

what helps you improve over time.

weightincrease gradually
repetitionswork within the listed ranges
techniquecontrolled throughout
rest between sets1–2 minutes
effortusually leave 2–3 good reps
sleepaim for 8–10 hours

don’t rush to heavier weights. when an exercise feels comfortable at the top of its rep range, increase the load slightly.

stick with the programme, record your weights and reps, eat enough to support growth and prioritise recovery. consistency over months is what makes the difference. 💪

equipment and safety

available: two 2.5 kg dumbbells and two 1 kg dumbbells. use controlled reps and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort. don’t grip two dumbbells together if they can slip. a sturdy, stable chair is only for support when it cannot tip or slide.

good form matters more than doing heaps of reps. take your rest days and stop if something hurts.
